# PMO Build Roadmap

## 1. Intake and triage
Build the first portal that captures demand in one place. Keep the first version small: request type, business unit, sponsor, problem, benefit, complexity, risk, and decision.

## 2. Charter approval
Create a charter record from approved intake. Add scope, out of scope, objectives, success measures, assumptions, dependencies, and sponsor approval.

## 3. Stage gates
Define standard lifecycle stages and gate criteria. Generate criteria checklists for each project stage and require evidence before approval.

## 4. Change, risk, and issue control
Add operational controls once projects are moving. Track owners, due dates, severity, decisions, escalations, and closure evidence.

## 5. Communications and reporting
Create status update records, milestone tracking, and Power BI report pages for executives and delivery teams.

## Suggested rollout
Start with SharePoint for speed if your team is small. Choose Dataverse when you need stronger role security, relationships, audit, environment strategy, or managed solution deployment.
